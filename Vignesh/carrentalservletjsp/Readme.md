# Car Rental System

The Car Rental System aims to simplify the process of renting vehicles, offering customers a convenient platform to book cars for their transportation needs. It provides real-time availability of vehicles, pricing options, and booking management features.


## Features

- **Display Available Cars**: Present a list of available cars with details such as make, model, year, and rental rates.
- **Manage Fleet**: Maintain a fleet of cars with varying types (sedan, SUV, luxury, etc.) and quantities.
- **Offer Rental Packages**: Provide rental packages with options for hourly, daily, weekly, and monthly rentals.
- **Provide Car Details**: Display comprehensive information about each car, including features, specifications, and availability.
- **Booking**: Allow users to book cars for specific dates and times, with the option to make reservations in advance.
- **Payment Options**: Offer multiple payment methods, including credit/debit cards, cash, and digital wallets.
- **Booking Management**: Enable users to view  their bookings.

## User Roles

1. **Administrator**: Responsible for managing the car inventory, rental rates, and user interactions.
2. **Customer**: Registered users who can search, book, view, and manage car rentals online.
3. **Guest**: Unregistered users who can search for available cars and view rental options.
